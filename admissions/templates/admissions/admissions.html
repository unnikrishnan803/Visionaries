Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common admission questions

When is the application deadline?

The application deadline for the upcoming academic year is June 30th. Early applications are highly encouraged as we review applications on a rolling basis. Late applications may be considered on a case-by-case basis.

Is there an application fee?

Yes, there is a non-refundable application fee of $50. This fee covers the administrative costs of processing your application. Payment can be made securely online during the application process.

Can I apply for multiple courses?

Yes, you can apply for up to 3 different courses in a single application. Please indicate your course preferences in order of priority. This allows us to consider you for multiple programs based on your qualifications.

How long does the admission process take?

The typical processing time is 2-4 weeks from the date of application submission. You will receive email notifications at each stage of the process. International applications may take slightly longer due to additional verification requirements.
